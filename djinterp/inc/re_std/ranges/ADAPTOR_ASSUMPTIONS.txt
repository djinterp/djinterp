==============================================================================
 INTERFACE ASSUMPTIONS FOR THE 2026-08-13 <ranges> ADAPTOR BATCH
==============================================================================

The adaptors added in this batch were written WITHOUT sight of the existing
<ranges> implementation. Everything they lean on is listed here, in one place,
so that reconciling them with the real tree is a single mechanical pass rather
than a hunt through seven files.

Every assumption below is inferred from the names already catalogued on the
<ranges> data sheet (65 of 78 symbols shipped, Phases R1-R29), so the NAMES are
known to be right. What is assumed is their SHAPE.

  ASSUMED SHAPE                              INFERRED FROM
  -----------------------------------------  ------------------------------
  iterator_t<R>          alias template      sheet lists it at C++11, and
  sentinel_t<R>          alias template      alias templates are C++11
  range_reference_t<R>   alias template
  range_difference_t<R>  alias template
  range_value_t<R>       alias template

  range<R>::value        TRAIT, not concept  sheet lists the concept layer at
  input_range<R>::value  TRAIT               C++11; C++20 `concept` cannot
  forward_range<R>::value                    exist there, so these must be
  sized_range<R>::value                      trait-shaped with ::value
  common_range<R>::value
  view<R>::value

  view_interface<Derived>       CRTP base    sheet, "Core types"
  range_adaptor_closure<D>      CRTP base    sheet, "Pipe machinery"
  ranges::begin/end/size        CPO objects  sheet, "Range access CPOs"
  views::all / all_view<R>      adaptor      sheet, "Range adaptors (C++20)"

IF ANY OF THESE IS SPELLED DIFFERENTLY, the fix is a rename in these files:
  elements_view.hpp  zip_view.hpp  enumerate_view.hpp  common_view.hpp
  range_common_reference.hpp

NOT ASSUMED, and deliberately so: none of the new adaptors uses
movable_box, non_propagating_cache, subrange, or the C++20 iterator concepts.
Each of those would have been a further guess, and each was avoidable - the
views here store their bases directly and define their own iterator/sentinel
pairs. That is why the assumption list is five entries long rather than twenty.
